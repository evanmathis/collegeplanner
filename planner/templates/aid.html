Paying for college

How financial aid works for this list
  1. FAFSA or CA Dream Act Application. Opens Oct 1. File one (not both) and list every school. It decides federal grants (Pell), Cal Grant, and each campus's own grants. File early; some aid runs out.
  2. Cal Grant GPA. Your high school sends your GPA to the state. Confirm with the counselor that it's sent by March 2.
  3. March 2 is the Cal Grant deadline and the priority aid date for every UC and CSU here. Treat it as the real deadline.
  4. Application fee waivers. UC and Cal State Apply each check eligibility inside the application based on family income. UC covers up to four campuses. Some music supplements (UCLA's Acceptd fee) are separate.
  5. Compare offers. After admission each school sends an aid offer. Log questions about them on the Questions page.
