Option 1: Subscribe (stays up to date)

Your calendar app checks the planner every few hours, so new dates and changes show up on their own and finished items drop off.

  • iPhone / iPad / Mac: Subscribe in Apple Calendar
    Or in Calendar on a Mac: File › New Calendar Subscription, and paste the address below. Choose "iCloud" as the location so it shows on every device.
  • Google Calendar: Other calendars › + › From URL, and paste the address below.

A subscription appears as its own calendar named "College Planner", next to Family. Calendar apps can't merge a subscription into an existing calendar. You can give it the same color as Family and share the subscription address with the rest of the family.
