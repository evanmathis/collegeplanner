Search sites

All free. Use a separate email address; they send a lot of marketing.

  • Venice High scholarship list (Naviance) Best odds: local Rotary, Kiwanis, PTA and credit-union awards. Few students apply.
  • BigFuture (College Board)
  • Fastweb
  • Scholarships.com
  • Going Merry
  • CareerOneStop (US Dept. of Labor) Opens in a browser only

Watch out for scams

  • Never pay to apply, to "hold" an award, or to file the FAFSA. The FAFSA is free.
  • No real scholarship is "guaranteed".
  • "You're a finalist" for something Cian never entered is a scam.
  • Skip paid search services and financial-aid seminars.
  • "No-essay" sweepstakes mostly collect contact details; the odds are a lottery.
