# More schools: notes (2026-10-07)

Rows are in `more_schools.csv`. "verified" means checked on the school's own page today; "partly verified" means the program is confirmed but some dates or costs are inferred; "inferred" means from typical past cycles, so confirm before relying on it.

## Money
- Cal Grant only pays at California schools (USC, Chapman, CalArts, SFCM, Pacific, Redlands). It does not follow Cian out of state, to Puerto Rico, or abroad.
- Pell Grants and federal loans work at the Puerto Rico Conservatory, because Puerto Rico is US territory.
- Pell is not usable at foreign schools. Federal Direct Loans can be used only at foreign schools on the Federal Student Aid foreign-school list; none of the abroad schools here were confirmed on it. In practice, plan abroad costs without US aid.
- The upside abroad is that public tuition is very low or free (Spain ~650-1,950 EUR/yr after subsidy, UNAM and UNA nearly free), so living costs are the main bill.
- Private US sticker prices (~75-95k/yr all-in) are rarely what families pay; run each school's Net Price Calculator.

## Applying abroad, in short
- Everything is in Spanish: entrance exams, interviews and classes. Cian would need solid academic Spanish plus music theory vocabulary in Spanish.
- Spain: an in-person entrance exam (prueba de acceso) in May-June, homologation of his US diploma to the Spanish Bachiller (start in spring 2027; it takes months), and a student visa from the Spanish consulate in Los Angeles.
- Mexico (UNAM): revalidation of his diploma, an in-person exam in Mexico City in spring, and a Mexican student visa.
- Colombia (Javeriana): apostilled diploma, Saber 11 or an accepted equivalent, and a student visa; an August start lines up with fall.
- Chile and Argentina start their year in March, so fall 2027 would mean a gap semester and a March 2028 start.
- Every abroad option needs his diploma and transcript apostilled (California Secretary of State).

## Considered and left out
- ESMUC (Barcelona): teaching is mainly in Catalan.
- Berklee Valencia: graduate programs only.

## Spain and Mexico (Evan's preferred countries)

Ranked for a composer starting in fall 2027:

**Spain** (degrees run Sept to June; conservatory degrees are official bachelor's-level Grados)
1. Real Conservatorio Superior de Musica de Madrid: the flagship conservatory, with the most composition faculty.
2. Musikene (San Sebastian): modern and well funded, with sign-up mid-March to mid-May and exams in June.
3. TAI (Madrid, private, Universidad Rey Juan Carlos degree): the pick if he leans toward film, media or contemporary styles.
4. COSCYL (Salamanca): a cheaper student city with a full composition program.
5. Valencia (Joaquin Rodrigo): strong music culture, but some teaching may be in Valencian.

**Mexico** (school year starts in August, so it lines up with fall)
1. UNAM Facultad de Musica (Mexico City): the top program, but few spots for external applicants because most students come up through UNAM's own 3-year prep program.
2. UANL (Monterrey): a clear composition track, low fees and a straightforward spring exam schedule. Probably the most realistic Mexican option.
3. Conservatorio Nacional de Musica (Mexico City): historic, but only 2 first-year composition students were admitted in 2026.

### Shared timeline for Spain and Mexico (fall 2027)
- Now to Dec 2026: get his US transcript and, once he graduates, his diploma apostilled by the California Secretary of State. Start building a portfolio of 3 works with scores and recordings, which works for both countries.
- Feb 2027: UNAM registration. Mar-Apr 2027: UANL registration and music exams.
- Spring 2027: apply for recognition of his diploma in Spain as soon as it can be filed. Spanish schools accept the application receipt for the entrance exam.
- May-June 2027: entrance exams in Spain and Mexico, all in person. That means trips to Spain and to Mexico City or Monterrey.
- June-July 2027: results, then the student visa from the consulate in Los Angeles.
- Aug (Mexico) or Sept (Spain) 2027: classes start.
